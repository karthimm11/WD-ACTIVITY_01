🎟️ Ticket Stand Categories & Perks

  • General Stand (₹700 – ₹900)
    Standard stadium tier seating, unobstructed view of giant screens, free water refill stations, and open public food stalls.
  • Premium Stand (₹1,500 – ₹2,000)
    Reserved mid-tier shaded seating, fast-track entry turnstiles, complimentary official team flag, and dedicated concession stands.
  • VIP Corporate Hospitality (₹3,800 – ₹5,000)
    Air-conditioned luxury lounge, padded balcony front-row seats, unlimited 5-star buffet meals & mocktails, and exclusive VIP parking pass.

📋 Key Booking & Entry Policies

  • 📲
    Instant SMS & QR Pass: Once booked, an instant confirmation SMS with unique Booking ID & QR entry code is delivered to your registered phone number.
  • ⏰
    Stadium Gate Timings: Gates open 3 hours before match toss (4:30 PM for night games). Entry strictly closes at the end of the 10th over of the 1st innings.
  • 🔢
    Ticket Purchase Limit: Maximum of 10 tickets per customer (per mobile number/email) to prevent scalping and ensure fair fan distribution.
  • 🔄
    Rain & Cancellation Policy: 100% full refund is initiated automatically if a match is abandoned without a single ball bowled.
